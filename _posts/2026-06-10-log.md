Welcome to **_log**.

The name is intentional. It's "blog" but the "b" is blank. Technically, it's just a **log**.

From a software engineering perspective, this feels right. We don't just "blog"; we create logs. Logs of thoughts, logs of patterns, logs of programs.

There is a growing trend, captured in a recent tweet, that suggests the latest programming language is English. If we follow that thread, then everything we write—every thought we capture, every pattern we document—can be seen as a program in its own right.

This space is dedicated to those logs. Whether they are snippets of code, architectural musings, or just the day-to-day logs of a developer's mind, they are all part of the process.

Welcome to the log.
